怎麼玩

一班夜車，三到十位乘客。每人暗中屬於鐘樓會或印信社，有一個行當（一項特技）和一件行李。鐘樓會要集齊三只懷錶，印信社要三枚玉印，而且攤牌時得說得出東西在哪個同伴手上。說對，全會贏；說錯，對面贏。

開車前

  • 幫會牌對半分。單數人數時多發一張、抽掉一張，所以有一邊會多一人，沒人知道是哪邊。人少的一邊每人有一杯烈酒，攤牌時可當一件（一次只算一杯）。
  • 每人一個行當，蓋著。剩下的行當放一旁，風衣可以去換。
  • 行李共 21 件：懷錶 3、玉印 3、皮箱 2，其餘各 1。兩只皮箱一定發出去，加上人數減二件其他行李，洗勻後每人一件；剩下的是牌堆。三人局每人兩件。
  • 三人局沒有黑函，十人局沒有風衣。

交換

暗中遞一件行李給任何一人。對方看過後，收下並回你一件，或拒絕。別人看不到換了什麼。換出去的行李如果印著「換出去時……」，效果就發動，並且會唸出來讓全車知道（走私變體除外）。黑函和破鏡不能拒收。皮箱不能和另一只皮箱互換。

動手

  1. 點名一人。神父此時可以攔下。
  2. 攻方或守方若是槍手，可以宣告誰都不准幫。
  3. 從攻方左手邊起，其他人依序表態：幫攻、幫守或旁觀。
  4. 攻方若是催眠師，點名一人旁觀，那人這場也不能用行當和行李。
  5. 大家同時亮出有用的行李與行當：匕首、皮手套、毒戒、飛刀、手杖；打手、武師、保鏢；藥劑師此時判定誰贏。
  6. 算：攻方一票加幫攻的人數加加成，守方同理。多的贏。平手時毒戒的一方贏，否則攻方從牌堆抽一件。
  7. 醫生此時可以取消結果。
  8. 贏家二選一：看輸家的幫會與行當，或翻輸家全部行李拿走一件。
  9. 拿走的若是輸家最後一件行李，贏家要從自己的行李挑一件還他（哪一件都行，連剛拿到的也可以），誰都不會空手。搬運工自己挑出最後一件、交出的金條是最後一件，也一樣。

攤牌

你手上要有至少一件自己幫會的目標物。說出你的幫會，攤開自己的一切，點名持有其餘目標物的同伴和各幾件。被點名的人攤開幫會與行李。全對，你們全會獲勝，沒被點名的同伴也算。錯一項，對面獲勝。持有黑函的人不能攤牌。

持有頭等票且自己手上有任意三件懷錶或玉印，可以直接獨自下車，不必點名任何人。烈酒不算。

牌堆空了之後，皮箱就是它印著的那件目標物。

擴充：車上人員

開車前勾選才加入。牌堆多一件金條，行當多三個，和原本十個一起洗。

金條
你在衝突中輸了時，可以把金條交給贏家：他拿走金條，不能再看、也不能拿別的。
雙面人
一局一次：有人要看你的幫會牌時（單片鏡、衝突勝方看牌），可以讓他看到另一邊；他看到的行當也會是盒子裡的另一張。
搬運工
行李上限 +2。衝突輸了、贏家要拿你一件行李時，由你挑給哪一件，這時翻開行當。
賭徒
身為攻方或守方，大家表態後擲硬幣：+0 或 +2。

開了擴充，每個衝突輸家都會被問要不要交金條，每個被看幫會牌的人都會被問要不要說謊，所以停頓不會洩漏誰有這兩張；手上沒有的人，畫面替他回答。

站站有事

開車前勾選才加入。第二站起，每過一站抽一張事件卡：六張事件、六張無事，抽完重洗，所以大約一半的站什麼也不會發生。

餐車
牌堆頂兩張翻開給全車看，仍留在原位、順序不變。
海關驗關
每人同時挑一件蓋著交給左鄰看過，原件還回。空手的人跳過，效果不觸發，也不算一次交換。
鍋爐加壓
這一站的衝突，攻方 +1。
暗號
全員同時指一個人（不能指自己）。被指最多的人從行李裡挑一件亮給全車看；平手就沒事。
車廂廣播
公布牌堆裡還剩幾只懷錶、幾枚玉印，以及幾只皮箱。
熄燈
這一站衝突的贏家只能看，不能拿。平手照舊抽牌。

鍋爐加壓與熄燈整站有效，車次列會一直掛著。

變體

走私：換出去的行李效果，由給的人決定要不要唸出來；不唸就不發動，也沒人知道換了什麼。

How to play

One night train, three to ten passengers. Everyone secretly belongs to the Clocktower Society or the Seal Society, has a trade with one trick to it, and a piece of luggage. The Clocktower Society need three pocket watches, the Seal Society three jade seals, and when you show your hand you must say whose bags the rest are in. Right, and your whole gang wins. Wrong, and the other gang does.

Before departure

  • Gang cards come in equal halves. At odd counts one extra card is shuffled in and one is left out unseen, so one gang is a member bigger and nobody knows which. Each member of the smaller gang holds a strong drink that counts as one item when showing a hand (one drink per claim).
  • One trade each, face down. The rest stay aside; the trench coat can fetch one.
  • Twenty-one pieces of luggage: 3 watches, 3 seals, 2 suitcases, one of everything else. Both suitcases are always dealt, along with two fewer other items than there are passengers; shuffled, one each. The rest is the pile. Two each at three players.
  • No poison-pen letter at three players, no trench coat at ten.

Trade

Hand one bag, face down, to anyone. They look at it and either keep it and hand one back, or refuse. Nobody else sees what moved. If the bag you gave away says "when you trade it on…", that fires, and it is read out to the carriage (unless smuggling is on). The poison-pen letter and the broken mirror cannot be refused. A suitcase is never traded for the other suitcase.

Scuffle

  1. Name someone. The Priest may stop it here.
  2. If the attacker or defender is the Gunman, they may bar all support.
  3. From the attacker's left, everyone else declares: back the attacker, back the defender, or stay out.
  4. If the attacker is the Hypnotist, they name one passenger who sits out and may use nothing this scuffle.
  5. Everyone shows what applies at once: dagger, gloves, poison ring, knives, cane; Thug, Master, Bodyguard; the Pharmacist rules now.
  6. Count: one for the attacker plus backers plus bonuses, likewise the defender. More wins. A tie goes to the poison ring's side, else the attacker draws from the pile.
  7. The Doctor may cancel the outcome here.
  8. The winner picks one: see the loser's gang and trade, or look through the loser's bags and take one.
  9. If that was the loser's last bag, the winner gives one of their own back (any one, even the bag just taken), so nobody is ever empty-handed. The same goes for a Porter handing over their last bag, or a gold bar that was all the loser had.

Show your hand

You need at least one of your gang's items yourself. Name your gang, reveal everything of yours, and name the allies who hold the rest and how many each. They reveal gang and bags. All right, and every member of your gang wins, named or not. Anything wrong, and the other gang wins. The poison-pen letter's holder may not show their hand.

With the first-class ticket and any three watches or seals in your own bag you may leave the train alone, naming nobody. The drink does not count.

Once the pile is empty, a suitcase is the item printed on it.

Expansion: the train crew

Switched on before departure. The deck gets a gold bar, and three more trades are shuffled in with the ten.

Gold bar
When you lose a scuffle, you may hand it to the winner instead: they take it and may neither look nor take anything else.
Double Agent
Once a game: when someone looks at your gang card (a monocle, or a scuffle winner's look), show them the other gang if you like; the trade they see is another one from the box.
Porter
Bag limit +2. When a scuffle winner takes one of your bags, you choose which, revealing your trade.
Gambler
As attacker or defender, after everyone has declared, toss a coin: +0 or +2.

With the expansion on, every scuffle loser is asked about the gold bar and everyone whose gang card is looked at is asked whether to lie, so a pause gives nothing away; for those without the card, the screen answers.

Something at every stop

Switched on before departure. From the second stop on, a card is drawn as each stop begins: six events and six blanks, reshuffled when the deck runs out, so about half the stops pass quietly.

Dining car
The top two cards of the pile are turned face up for everyone and stay where they are.
Customs check

Boiler up

Passwords
Everyone points at somebody at once (not themselves). Whoever is pointed at most shows one bag to the carriage; a tie means nothing happens.
Announcement
How many watches, seals and suitcases are left in the pile is read out.
Lights out
This stop a scuffle's winner may only look, never take. Ties still draw.

Boiler up and lights out last the whole stop, so the bar keeps showing them.

Variant

Smuggling: a traded card's text fires only if the giver reads it out; otherwise it changes hands silently.
